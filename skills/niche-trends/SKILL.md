# Niche trends with SFOM.AI

Call `get_trend_report` with a niche slug: real-estate, beauty, auto, tech,
finance, business, education, fitness, food, fashion, health, parenting,
comedy, music, gaming, art, ecom. If the user's niche is not listed, pick the
closest one and say so.

The report ranks videos by views ÷ followers, small-but-viral first. For each
of the top items the tool returns the creator, views, the coefficient and,
where analysed, the hook.

Present:

1. Three to five formats that recur across different creators — a trend is a
   format two or more unrelated accounts broke out with, not one lucky post.
2. For each format: one real example (handle, views, multiple) and the hook
   mechanism, then how the user would shoot it in their own voice. Copy the
   mechanism, never the transcript.
3. Who is rising: creators whose recent posts run above their own norm — the
   ones to study or to book before their rate card catches up.

Do not pad with generic advice. Every claim must trace to a number the tool
returned. Public hub for sharing: `https://www.sfom.ai/report/trend/<niche>`;
real hooks by niche: `https://www.sfom.ai/tools/instagram-hooks`.
