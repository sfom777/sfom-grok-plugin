---
description: Vet a creator before you pay them — measured stats and a CPM from your budget
argument-hint: <platform> <@handle> [budget in USD]
---

Use the SFOM.AI `get_creator_report` tool for the platform and handle in
`$ARGUMENTS` (strip the @). Then follow the `creator-vetting` skill: verdict
first, then median views, reach per follower, engagement by views, own-norm
multiple, cadence. If a budget is given, compute CPM = budget ÷ (median views
÷ 1000) and compare it with paid-social CPMs for the same audience. Finish with
the public report link.
